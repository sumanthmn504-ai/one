About the project

More than a game.

Sports Connect – Karnataka is a college ASIP project demonstration prototype exploring how a simple digital platform could support Physical Education (PE) in schools.

It brings students, school heads, PE teachers, NGOs, sponsors and equipment suppliers into a shared experience. All sample statistics, people, events, requests and prices in this prototype are illustrative—not official or verified records.
